Swipe Scouting

Coaches discover verified athletes fast, inside NCAA compliance windows only.

Browse athlete profiles with a fast, familiar swipe — like, save, or pass. Every card is sport-filtered and shows verified stats, highlight video, and academics at a glance.
  • Filter by sport, position, grad year, and region
  • Contact windows auto-locked to NCAA rules — no accidental violations
  • Save shortlists and revisit them as the recruiting window opens

Athlete Protection

Every uploaded video is automatically watermarked. Visibility is opt-in by default.

Protecting young athletes is built into the core, not bolted on.
  • Automatic, non-removable watermark on every uploaded video
  • Timestamped consent acknowledgment required before each upload
  • Profiles are invisible to pro teams, agents, and media until the athlete opts in — per league
  • Parent or guardian co-accounts for athletes under the age of majority

Turn interest into insight. Each week, athletes get a clear report of recruiting activity on their profile.
  • Which programs viewed you, and how often
  • Time spent and which sections drew the most attention
  • Trends over time so you know where momentum is building

Verified Identity

Multi-step verification for athletes and institutions before any access is granted.

Trust starts with knowing everyone is who they say they are.

  • Institutions complete multi-step verification before gaining scouting access
  • Agents must maintain valid union certification
  • Instant access revocation if credentials lapse
