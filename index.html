Swipe Scouting

Coaches discover verified athletes fast, inside NCAA compliance windows only.

Browse athlete profiles with a fast, familiar swipe. Like, save, or pass. Every card is filtered by sport and shows verified stats, highlight video, and academics at a glance.
  • Filter by sport, position, grad year, and region
  • Contact windows lock to NCAA rules automatically, so no accidental violations
  • Save shortlists and revisit them as the recruiting window opens

Athlete Protection

Every uploaded video is automatically watermarked. Visibility is off until you opt in.

Protecting young athletes is built into the core, not bolted on.
  • Automatic, permanent watermark on every uploaded video
  • Timestamped consent acknowledgment required before each upload
  • Profiles are invisible to pro teams, agents, and media until the athlete opts in, per league
  • Parent or guardian accounts for athletes under the age of majority

Verified Identity

Multi-step verification for athletes and institutions before any access is granted.
